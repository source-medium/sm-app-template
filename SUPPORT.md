# Getting help

- **Your SourceMedium data, the Apps page, keys, or query allowances:** open a
  support request from your SourceMedium workspace
  (https://sourcemedium.com/docs/product-tours/get-support) or email
  support@sourcemedium.com.
- **A bug in this template:** open an issue on this repository with the steps
  to reproduce and the output of `pnpm diagnose`. Never paste a key, password,
  or `.env.local` into an issue.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

SourceMedium maintains this template and the integration patterns in it, not
the changes in your copy.
